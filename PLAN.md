# Research paper: human text in the "AI style region"

**Author of record:** the student (verifies, runs code, submits). **Writer:** Claude (AI), fully disclosed.
**Field:** computational linguistics. **Budget:** $0, local PC (RX 7800 XT 16GB, 32GB RAM).

## Research question
To what extent does human-written text occupy the same measurable style region as LLM-generated text,
and did human writing in arXiv abstracts move further into that region after Nov 2022 than its
pre-2022 trend predicts (vs. a less-exposed control corpus)?
